# ETF Strategy — Stakeholder Brief

## Business Question
Can recent SPY market information help predict next-day returns?

## Why It Matters
If historical market information provides useful predictive signals, it may help inform short-term portfolio decisions.

## What We Will Do
- Collect historical SPY price and volume data.
- Create features from recent market information.
- Build a simple predictive model.
- Compare the model with a baseline.
- Evaluate performance and quantify uncertainty.

## Decision
Determine whether the model provides useful predictive information for short-term decision-making.
